# Changelog

## Unreleased

### New

- Files you opened from another app say clearly why they can't be opened again, and what you can do: allow their
  folder, open the folder in a file manager, pick the file, or open it again from the app that sent it.
- "Remove from Recents" on any recent file. Your place, highlights and notes are kept.
- The header turns into a floating pill as you scroll, and so does the search box.
- Buttons, chips, tabs and cards react to your finger, and shapes change softly when you select something.
- Switching tabs slides the whole page, and you can drag between tabs.
- Haptic feedback (off until you turn it on), and more colour families.

### Improved

- Big text files, long books and large Word files open at once instead of freezing the app.
- Changing the text size, turning pages and switching to Reader mode are much faster.
- Reading a long book uses less than half the memory.
- The first visit to each tab, filters, menus and sheets are smoother.
- Every row in Settings and More says what it holds. Every menu row has an icon.
- The welcome screens are shorter, with one setup page.
- The app follows your phone for motion: no movement when animations are off, less on phones with little memory.

### Fixed

- A recent file no longer says "Can't find this file" when Unfurl only lost access to it.
- Home shows your latest place as soon as you close a book.
- Word files no longer break pages where the document doesn't.
- "End of chapter" shows on the last page of each chapter.
- The search box can be tapped anywhere, not only on its text.
- Switches that are off no longer look disabled.
